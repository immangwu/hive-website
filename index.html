About HIVE

Hub For Innovation And Entrepreneurship

HIVE at SRIT is a dynamic ecosystem built to transform student ideas into impactful, real-world products. We empower students with everything they need to innovate — cutting-edge facilities, expert mentorship, skill development programs, funding access, and powerful networking opportunities.

At HIVE, we don’t just support innovation — we fuel it. Our mission is to equip the next generation of changemakers with the resources, guidance, and confidence to drive meaningful technological advancements.

Vision

To foster a thriving ecosystem that provides essential support and facilities for nurturing innovative ideas from conception to impactful viable products.

Mission

To empower students with comprehensive resources, essential skills, access to funding, expert mentorship, networking and cutting-edge facilities.

What HIVE offers

Where industry problems meet real-time solutions

Three pillars that take a student from problem-solving skills to a product on the shop floor.

01

Skill Development Classes

A structured problem-solving methodology that teaches students to tackle real-world challenges with empathy-driven design, plus curated upskilling courses aligned with industry demand.

02

Industry Problem Statements

Multidisciplinary faculty teams receive live problems from industry partners and solve them in real time alongside students.

03

Product Development

Students build industry-relevant products in manufacturing, automation and AI: visual inspection, AI assembly guidance, digital twins and IoT production dashboards.
